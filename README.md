## About me

Hello! My name is Arohee (pronounced AH-roh-hee), I use any pronouns, and I'm a handpoke tattoo artist based in Pittsburgh, PA until the end of July, and then New York City after that! 

Much of my work is whimsical and abstract, inspired by natural forms and vintage adornments. I have always found tattooing to be a form of liberation -- a reclamation of one's body and the way it is perceived, and a commitment to honoring one's own authentic self in a society that expects assimilation and privileges invisibility.

I am committed to providing a safe and comfortable environment for you to get tattooed -- this means cleanliness, safety (I am bloodborne pathogen certified), and honoring your bodily autonomy and consent.

## Booking

### Flash appointments
- All of my flash is repeatable (unless stated otherwise)! 
- I'm always happy to modify flash to your liking -- we can stack and stretch and mirror, invert, change the texture and lightness of fill, and/or edit the design to fit better in your desired placement or around existing tattoos.
- You are always allowed to change your mind about design, size, or placement, as long as it can be done in the time we have allotted or split up into multiple sessions. 

### Custom appointments
- Customs are one-of-one, but I'm happy to make something similar to past custom work!
- When booking a custom, describe your vision as specifically as you like. If you include reference images of other artists' work, please describe how you would like them to inspire our design (e.g. elements you like, color, texture, placement). It also helps if you include multiple pictures (think moodboard/vibe check rather than "I want this specific piece from this other artist"). 
- I generally do not send custom designs in advance, but we will have plenty of time to make changes during your appointment. You are always allowed to request edits!
- If significant edits are required, we will discuss and collaborate on design during the appointment, and then I'll schedule you in for another session to complete the tattoo.

### Pricing
- My minimum quote is $50 and my average quote is around $150-200. I am happy to provide pricing info prior to your appointment!
- Factors that go into pricing: size, complexity of design, color (colored inks take longer to poke into the skin than black ink), placement (torso placements are stretchy -- a 2-inch tattoo may end up being closer to 3 or 4 inches)
- Whenever I can, I'm happy to offer sliding scale appointments, work with budgets, and barter. If this is something you're interested in, please reach out!
- Tips are very appreciated but not at all required. I also appreciate non-monetary tips, especially handmade goods and sweet treats :)

### Trades
- I love trading with other local artists and makers! All I ask is that it's an ~even exchange of time and resources.
- Trades I'm especially interested in: tattoos, handmade clothing/jewelry, candles, ceramics, decor.
- Trades I do not accept: energy healing, tarot/astrology (it's just not my thing!)

### How long will it take?
- This is very dependent on size/placement/the piece itself. I try to include timing info in my Instagram captions (whenever I remember to time myself...) and I'm also happy to provide time estimates, although they may not always be accurate.
- A non-exhaustive list of factors that affect timing: design modifications, stenciling, size, placement, color vs. black, how quickly your skin takes ink (hydrated skin = faster tattooing!)
- Handpoke is a labor of love! Please do not schedule anything directly after your tattoo :)

### Late policy
- Life happens, I totally understand! But please give me a heads up if you know you will be more than ten minutes late, and if you're running more than half an hour late we may have to reschedule your appointment. 

### How should I prepare?
- Eat beforehand, stay well hydrated, and moisturize and gently exfoliate the area (don’t overdo it!) in the days leading up to your tattoo. Tattooing dry skin is like drawing on tissue paper — it’s much harder to deposit ink evenly. Hydrated skin = less irritation and quicker tattooing.
- Many substances and medications interact with the body in unexpected ways. Please let me know if you are on anything that may have blood-thinning effects, or that might affect the texture and hydration of your skin. 
- I cannot tattoo you if you are drunk or hungover, if you are sunburned, or if you are on Accutane.

## Aftercare

### Wrapping things up 
- For immediate aftercare, I can cover your tattoo either using Derm Shield (a waterproof second-skin adhesive bandage designed to protect your tattoo during the first 1-3 days of healing), or regular old plastic wrap. Derm Shield may irritate your skin if you are sensitive to adhesives, if it is applied onto a stretchy or sensitive placement, or if there's soap residue trapped under the bandage (I will wipe you down with distilled water to prevent this!)
- The choice is up to you based on your comfort, and does not affect the result :)

If healing with Derm Shield: If the bandage peels up to expose your tattoo or you notice any irritation, take it off immediately; otherwise, leave it on for 1-3 days. It's generally easiest to take the bandage off in the shower by pulling like a command strip.

If healing with plastic wrap: Leave it on for an hour or so.

### After removing the bandage
- Keep it clean! Wash your tattoo with gentle, unscented soap in the shower. Avoid contact with pets, submerging yourself in bodies of water, touching public gym equipment, etc. 
- Keep it moisturized! Use unscented lotion, Aquaphor, Vaseline, etc. to moisturize your tattoo if you notice itching, peeling, or flaking. Do not scratch or pick at your tattoo -- this may compromise the healing and cause ink to fall out.
- Keep it covered! Avoid direct sun exposure and keep your tattoo covered to prevent exposure to dirt and bacteria.

After your tattoo is healed (~3-4 weeks), protect it with sunscreen to avoid fading!

### Touch-ups
- Always free, no statute of limitations! I tattoo with a light hand, so ink may fall out during the healing process and need to be touched up. Reach out after your tattoo is fully healed (include a picture, please!) and we'll set something up.
